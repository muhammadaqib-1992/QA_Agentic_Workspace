## Why this folder matters to QA

A defect and a change request look identical from the outside: the system does something other than what you expected. The difference is whether the behaviour was ever agreed. Before filing, check whether the requirement exists here at all — filing a change request as a defect wastes a triage cycle and erodes trust in the queue.

## Known gaps
